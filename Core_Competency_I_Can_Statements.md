# BC Core Competencies — "I Can" Statements by Profile

This knowledge source lists student self-assessment "I can" statements for the three Core Competencies. Each statement belongs to a **Competency Area**, a **Sub-competency**, and a **Profile** (1–6). Profiles increase in complexity: Profile 1 is the simplest and Profile 6 is the most sophisticated. Each statement has a unique ID in the form `SUBCODE-P#-##`.

## How to Select a Statement

1. **Identify the Competency Area** from the request: Communication, Thinking, or Personal and Social.
2. **Identify the Sub-competency** that best matches the behaviour described (see the table below).
3. **Identify the Profile level** that matches the complexity of what the learner is doing (see Profile Levels). If the level is not stated, infer it from the evidence: simple, supported, concrete actions point to lower profiles; independent, strategic, evaluative, or community-impacting actions point to higher profiles.
4. **Choose the statement(s)** under that Area → Sub-competency → Profile that most closely match the evidence. Quote the statement exactly and include its ID.
5. If the evidence sits between two profiles, choose the lower profile that is fully demonstrated and name the next profile's statement as a next step.

## Profile Levels

| Profile | Complexity | General description |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Emerging / earliest | awareness, exploration, needs support |
| 2 | Beginning | simple, concrete actions in familiar settings |
| 3 | Developing | purposeful, independent use in familiar contexts |
| 4 | Proficient | deliberate, strategic, connects ideas and adapts |
| 5 | Extending | complex, flexible, evaluative; impacts others |
| 6 | Advanced | sophisticated, self-directed, transformative impact beyond the group |

## Competency Structure

| Competency Area | Sub-competency | ID prefix | Focus |
|---|---|---|---|
| Communication | Communicating | COM-CM | Expressing, listening, presenting, and acquiring information |
| Communication | Collaborating | COM-CO | Working with others, group roles, conflict, shared goals |
| Thinking | Creative Thinking | THK-CR | Generating and developing new ideas |
| Thinking | Critical and Reflective Thinking | THK-CT | Questioning, analyzing, judging, reflecting, using feedback and criteria |
| Personal and Social | Personal Awareness and Responsibility | PS-PA | Self-regulation, well-being, goals, self-advocacy |
| Personal and Social | Positive Personal and Cultural Identity | PS-PI | Self-identity, values, culture, strengths, belonging |
| Personal and Social | Social Awareness and Responsibility | PS-SA | Relationships, inclusion, fairness, community and environment |

# Competency Area: Communication

## Communication › Communicating

### Communication › Communicating › Profile 1

*Level: Emerging / earliest — awareness, exploration, needs support*

- **COM-CM-P1-01**: I can respond meaningfully to communication from peers and adults.

### Communication › Communicating › Profile 2

*Level: Beginning — simple, concrete actions in familiar settings*

- **COM-CM-P2-01**: I can communicate and listen to peers and adults by talking.
- **COM-CM-P2-02**: I can communicate for a purpose.
- **COM-CM-P2-03**: I can communicate information about topics that are important to me.
- **COM-CM-P2-04**: I can answer simple and direct questions about my experiences.

### Communication › Communicating › Profile 3

*Level: Developing — purposeful, independent use in familiar contexts*

- **COM-CM-P3-01**: I can consider my purpose when I am choosing a form and content.
- **COM-CM-P3-02**: I can communicate clearly about topics I know and understand well, using forms and strategies I have practiced.
- **COM-CM-P3-03**: I can gather the basic information I need and present it.
- **COM-CM-P3-04**: I can participate in conversations for a variety of purposes.
- **COM-CM-P3-05**: I can listen and respond to others.

### Communication › Communicating › Profile 4

*Level: Proficient — deliberate, strategic, connects ideas and adapts*

- **COM-CM-P4-01**: I can share my ideas and try to connect them with others' ideas.
- **COM-CM-P4-02**: I am an active listener and can make connections.
- **COM-CM-P4-03**: I am an active listener and can ask clarifying and extending questions when appropriate.
- **COM-CM-P4-04**: I can plan ways to make my message clear and engaging for my audience.
- **COM-CM-P4-05**: I can create communications that focus on a variety of purposes and audiences.
- **COM-CM-P4-06**: I can acquire the information that I need for specific tasks and for my own interests and present information clearly.

### Communication › Communicating › Profile 5

*Level: Extending — complex, flexible, evaluative; impacts others*

- **COM-CM-P5-01**: I can be an engaged listener and integrate new information from questions asked.
- **COM-CM-P5-02**: I can create a wide range of effective communications that feature powerful images and words.
- **COM-CM-P5-03**: I can identify ways to change my communications to make them effective for different audiences.
- **COM-CM-P5-04**: I can use my understanding of the role and impact of story to engage my audiences in meaning making.
- **COM-CM-P5-05**: I can acquire information about complex and specialized topics from various sources.
- **COM-CM-P5-06**: I can synthesize information from a variety of sources and present it with a thoughtful analysis.
- **COM-CM-P5-07**: I can focus and help build and extend understanding in discussions and conversations.
- **COM-CM-P5-08**: I can be an engaged listener and ask thought provoking questions when appropriate.

### Communication › Communicating › Profile 6

*Level: Advanced — sophisticated, self-directed, transformative impact beyond the group*

- **COM-CM-P6-01**: I can assess audience response and draw on a repertoire of strategies to increase my intended impact.
- **COM-CM-P6-02**: I can acquire, critically analyze, and integrate well-chosen information from a range of sources.
- **COM-CM-P6-03**: I can contribute purposefully to discussions and conversations.
- **COM-CM-P6-04**: I can synthesize, deepen, and transform my own and others' thinking.
- **COM-CM-P6-05**: I can weave multiple messages into my communications and I understand that my audience will use their own knowledge and experiences in making meaning.
- **COM-CM-P6-06**: I can show understanding and control of the forms and technologies I use.

## Communication › Collaborating

### Communication › Collaborating › Profile 1

*Level: Emerging / earliest — awareness, exploration, needs support*

- **COM-CO-P1-01**: I can participate with others.

### Communication › Collaborating › Profile 2

*Level: Beginning — simple, concrete actions in familiar settings*

- **COM-CO-P2-01**: I can contribute during group activities.
- **COM-CO-P2-02**: I can cooperate with others.
- **COM-CO-P2-03**: I can listen respectfully to other people's ideas.
- **COM-CO-P2-04**: I can work with others for a specific purpose.

### Communication › Collaborating › Profile 3

*Level: Developing — purposeful, independent use in familiar contexts*

- **COM-CO-P3-01**: I can take on different roles and tasks in the group and work respectfully and safely in our shared space.
- **COM-CO-P3-02**: I can express my ideas and help others feel comfortable to share theirs so that all voices are included.
- **COM-CO-P3-03**: I can work with others to achieve a common goal and can evaluate our group processes and results.

### Communication › Collaborating › Profile 4

*Level: Proficient — deliberate, strategic, connects ideas and adapts*

- **COM-CO-P4-01**: I can draw on past experiences to negotiate and develop group processes.
- **COM-CO-P4-02**: I can be an active listener and speaker.
- **COM-CO-P4-03**: I can share my ideas and try to connect them with others' ideas.
- **COM-CO-P4-04**: I can ask clarifying questions about others' ideas and check for understanding when appropriate.
- **COM-CO-P4-05**: I can test my ideas with others and consider their input.
- **COM-CO-P4-06**: I can help resolve conflicts and challenges as they arise.
- **COM-CO-P4-07**: I can recognize how my contributions and those of others complement each other.
- **COM-CO-P4-08**: I can plan with others and adjust our plan according to the group's purpose.
- **COM-CO-P4-09**: I can identify and apply roles and strategies to facilitate group work.

### Communication › Collaborating › Profile 5

*Level: Extending — complex, flexible, evaluative; impacts others*

- **COM-CO-P5-01**: I can disagree respectfully, and anticipate potential conflicts, and help manage them when they arise.
- **COM-CO-P5-02**: I can give, receive, and act on constructive feedback in support of our goals.
- **COM-CO-P5-03**: I can evaluate and revise plans with other group members.
- **COM-CO-P5-04**: I can play a role in collectively monitoring the progress of the group and adjust my contributions as needed.
- **COM-CO-P5-05**: I can recognize the interdependence of our roles and draw on these to move us forward.
- **COM-CO-P5-06**: I can ask thought-provoking questions, integrate new information and various perspectives from others, and think critically about whose voices are missing.

### Communication › Collaborating › Profile 6

*Level: Advanced — sophisticated, self-directed, transformative impact beyond the group*

- **COM-CO-P6-01**: I can step outside my comfort zone to develop working relationships with unfamiliar groups.
- **COM-CO-P6-02**: I can develop and coordinate networking partnerships beyond and in service of the group.
- **COM-CO-P6-03**: I can demonstrate my commitment to the group's purpose by taking on different roles as needed.
- **COM-CO-P6-04**: I can acknowledge different perspectives and seek out and create space for missing or marginalized voices.
- **COM-CO-P6-05**: I can summarize key themes to identify commonalities and focus on deepening or transforming our collective thinking and actions.
- **COM-CO-P6-06**: I can recognize when wisdom and strategies from others are needed and access these to address complex goals.
- **COM-CO-P6-07**: I help create connections with other groups or networks to further our common goals and our impact.

# Competency Area: Thinking

## Thinking › Creative Thinking

### Thinking › Creative Thinking › Profile 1

*Level: Emerging / earliest — awareness, exploration, needs support*

- **THK-CR-P1-01**: I can get ideas when I play.
- **THK-CR-P1-02**: I can get ideas when I use my senses to explore.
- **THK-CR-P1-03**: I can have fun when I use my ideas to play.
- **THK-CR-P1-04**: I can make my ideas work or I change what I am doing.

### Thinking › Creative Thinking › Profile 2

*Level: Beginning — simple, concrete actions in familiar settings*

- **THK-CR-P2-01**: I can have fun with my ideas.
- **THK-CR-P2-02**: I can get new ideas to create new things and/or solve a problem.
- **THK-CR-P2-03**: I can use my imagination to get new ideas of my own, or build on other's ideas, or combine other people's ideas in new ways.
- **THK-CR-P2-04**: I can make my ideas work when there is a constraint of a form, problem or materials.

### Thinking › Creative Thinking › Profile 3

*Level: Developing — purposeful, independent use in familiar contexts*

- **THK-CR-P3-01**: I can generate new ideas as I pursue my interests.
- **THK-CR-P3-02**: I can deliberately learn a lot about something by doing research, talking to others, or practicing, so that I can generate new ideas about it.
- **THK-CR-P3-03**: I can build the skills I need to make my ideas work, and I usually succeed, even if it takes a few tries.

### Thinking › Creative Thinking › Profile 4

*Level: Proficient — deliberate, strategic, connects ideas and adapts*

- **THK-CR-P4-01**: I can get ideas that are new to my peers.
- **THK-CR-P4-02**: I can use my creative ideas to express myself.
- **THK-CR-P4-03**: I can use strategies deliberately for quieting my mind so that I can be creative.
- **THK-CR-P4-04**: I can use my experiences with various steps and attempts to direct my future work.

### Thinking › Creative Thinking › Profile 5

*Level: Extending — complex, flexible, evaluative; impacts others*

- **THK-CR-P5-01**: I can get new ideas that are innovative, may not have been seen before, and have an impact on my peers or in my community.
- **THK-CR-P5-02**: I can have interests and passions that I pursue over time.
- **THK-CR-P5-03**: I can look for new perspectives, new problems, or new approaches.
- **THK-CR-P5-04**: I am willing to take significant risks in my thinking in order to generate lots of ideas.
- **THK-CR-P5-05**: I can accept ambiguity, setbacks, and failure, and I use them to advance the development of my ideas.

### Thinking › Creative Thinking › Profile 6

*Level: Advanced — sophisticated, self-directed, transformative impact beyond the group*

- **THK-CR-P6-01**: I can have strong commitment to a personal aesthetic and values, and the inner motivation to persevere over years if necessary, to develop my ideas.
- **THK-CR-P6-02**: I can get ideas that are ground-breaking or disruptive and can develop them to form a body of work over time that has an impact in my community or beyond.
- **THK-CR-P6-03**: I can challenge assumptions as a matter of course and have deliberate strategies for getting new ideas intuitively.

## Thinking › Critical and Reflective Thinking

### Thinking › Critical and Reflective Thinking › Profile 1

*Level: Emerging / earliest — awareness, exploration, needs support*

- **THK-CT-P2-07**: I can communicate something about my thinking.

- **THK-CT-P4-04**: I give, receive, and act on constructive feedback.
- **THK-CT-P4-05**: I can use what I know and observe to identify problems and ask questions.

### Thinking › Critical and Reflective Thinking › Profile 5

- **THK-CT-P5-07**: I can consider alternative approaches and make strategic choices.
- **THK-CT-P5-08**: I can take risks and recognize that I may not be immediately successful.
- **THK-CT-P5-09**: I can examine my thinking, seek feedback, reassess my work, and adjust.

### Thinking › Critical and Reflective Thinking › Profile 6

*Level: Advanced — sophisticated, self-directed, transformative impact beyond the group*

- **THK-CT-P6-01**: I can offer detailed analysis, using specific terminology of my progress, work, and goals.
- **THK-CT-P6-02**: I can determine my own framework and criteria for tasks that involve critical thinking.
- **THK-CT-P6-03**: I can compile evidence and draw reasoned conclusions.
- **THK-CT-P6-04**: I can consider perspectives that do not fit with my understandings.
- **THK-CT-P6-05**: I can be open-minded and patient, taking the time to explore, discover, and understand.
- **THK-CT-P6-06**: I can make choices that will help me create my intended impact on an audience or situation.
- **THK-CT-P6-07**: I can place my work and that of others in a broader context.
- **THK-CT-P6-08**: I can connect the results of my inquiries and analyses with action.
- **THK-CT-P6-09**: I can articulate a keen awareness of my strengths, my aspirations and how my experiences and contexts affect my frameworks and criteria.
